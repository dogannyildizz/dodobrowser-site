Privacy and sync

Your history stays on your device. Sync is your choice.

Your history and the passwords you save on Windows are stored on your device and aren't synced. Your data isn't sold or used for advertising.

On Windows you can sync your bookmarks, shortcuts and settings with your Google account, and see the tabs open on your other computers.

Problem reports, contact messages and what's shared with outside services are explained in the Privacy policy.

  • Your own Google Drive space: Synced data is stored in a hidden folder set aside for DodoBrowser.
  • Limited access: DodoBrowser can't see or change the other files in your Drive.
  • Optional sign-in: You can use the browser without signing in.
  • A tracker-free site: This site uses no cookies or analytics tools and loads nothing from outside.

Frequently asked questions

Before you download.

Is it free?

Yes. DodoBrowser is free, needs no account and doesn't show ads of its own.

Which versions of Windows does it run on?

It runs on 64-bit Windows 10 (version 1809 and later) and Windows 11. If the page engine it needs is missing, it helps you install it.

Can I install it without opening the Microsoft Store app?

Yes. The Microsoft installer is a small file; when you run it, it installs DodoBrowser without opening the Store app. You can also run this command in a terminal:

winget install 9P17GF8DSSL1 -s msstore

Both install the same package, signed by Microsoft, and updates still arrive on their own. Copies of an .exe or .msix found on other sites are not ours.

Will sites open properly?

DodoBrowser uses Edge's page engine on Windows and the phone's own on Android. Both are Chromium-based, like Chrome and Edge. If a site gives you trouble, you can turn the Shield off for that site.

Can I bring my Chrome bookmarks?

Yes. On Windows you can import them, folders included, from Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi and Opera. On Android, if you sign in with Google, your bookmarks from your computer come to your phone too.

Can I install extensions?

Not for now. Ad and tracker blocking and cookie banner refusal are built in.

How do updates arrive?

The Windows version updates itself through Microsoft Store. The page engine updates separately; DodoBrowser lets you know when a restart is needed. On Android, Dodo tells you about a new version in the menu; tap “Güncelle” (Update) and it downloads it itself, and you approve the install in Android's window. Your bookmarks and settings are kept.

Is there an Android version?

Yes. For Android 10 and later, you can download it as an APK file from the Android page. It isn't on Google Play; it's free and needs no account. The Android app's interface is Turkish only for now.

Is DodoBrowser the same as Dodo Browser for Mac?

No, they're separate products. DodoBrowser is for Windows and Android, and its site is dodobrowser.com. There's no Mac version for now.

Why DodoBrowser?

It brings together an English and Turkish interface, a built-in Shield and on-device translation. It's designed for everyday use.
